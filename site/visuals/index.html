    VISUALS

    This guide intentionally avoids including texture mods, with a few exceptions. Retextures are highly subjective, and what looks good to me may not suit another. This subjectivity extends to performance as well: opting for high resolution textures across the board may look great, but can significantly impact performance on lower end systems. Additionally, mixing retextures often leads to visual inconsistencies, where untouched objects stand out against retextured ones. For these reasons, textures are best left to personal preference and tailored to your own system.

    Create a separator

    Right-click in the left pane of MO2 and select Create separator. Name it VISUALS.
