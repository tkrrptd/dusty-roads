<p>Just like <span class="highlight">ENB</span> or <span class="highlight">ReShade</span>, <span class="highlight">NVR</span> can be tweaked to match your preferred visual style. You can either adjust the settings yourself or download a preset, both of which can be found on <span class="highlight">Nexus Mods</span> and the <span class="highlight">NVR Discord server</span> (<code>#presets-forum</code>). Most available presets are designed for vanilla <span class="highlight">New Vegas</span> however, typically with <span class="highlight">Desert Natural Weathers</span> in mind. Fortunately, a member of the <span class="highlight">DUST Discord server</span> was kind enough to share a preset created specifically for <span class="highlight">DUST</span>:</p>
